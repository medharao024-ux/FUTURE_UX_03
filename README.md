---

## 🌐 Project Overview

**NOVA CRM** is a modern **Client Relationship Management (CRM) dashboard** designed for small agencies and B2B service-based businesses.

The system provides a centralized workspace where teams can manage the complete customer lifecycle — from the first lead interaction to client onboarding, project management and follow-up activities.

Instead of switching between multiple tools, users can access important client and business information from a single dashboard.

---

## 💡 Problem Statement

Agency teams often manage information across different spreadsheets, communication platforms and task-management tools.

This can make it difficult to:

- Track potential leads
- Understand the current sales pipeline
- Maintain client information
- Remember follow-up activities
- Monitor project progress
- Identify clients requiring attention
- Keep track of team activities

### NOVA CRM solves this by providing a single structured workspace for these activities.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of NOVA CRM are:

**01. Centralize Information**  
Keep leads, clients, projects and tasks in one place.

**02. Improve Lead Tracking**  
Allow teams to monitor leads throughout the sales process.

**03. Visualize Sales Progress**  
Use a pipeline-based interface to understand the current status of opportunities.

**04. Improve Client Management**  
Provide detailed client profiles containing important relationship information.

**05. Manage Follow-ups**  
Track tasks, deadlines, priorities and assigned team members.

**06. Improve Visibility**  
Provide dashboards and activity information to understand ongoing work.

**07. Improve Usability**  
Create a clean and easy-to-navigate interface for everyday agency workflows.

---

# ✦ Core Features

## 1. 📊 Dashboard

The dashboard provides an overview of the current CRM status.

### Includes:

- Key performance indicators
- Lead statistics
- Client statistics
- Sales pipeline summary
- Opportunity trends
- Today's tasks
- Recent activities

The dashboard helps users understand **what is happening across the CRM at a glance**.

---

# 2. 👤 Lead Management

The Leads section allows users to organize and monitor potential customers.

### Lead information includes:

- Lead name
- Company
- Email
- Phone number
- Lead source
- Deal value
- Lead status
- Priority
- Assigned owner
- Last contact

### Lead actions:

- Search leads
- Filter leads
- Add new leads
- View lead information
- Track lead status
- Assign ownership
